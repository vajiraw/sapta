A taste of Ceylon

Ceylon Black Pepper

Ceylon black pepper, harvested from the tropical vines, is world-renowned for its exceptionally high piperine content and intense, biting pungency. Unlike standard varieties, it offers a complex flavor profile rich with bold citrus, floral, and woody undertones. The peppercorns are traditionally hand-picked and sun-dried to develop their characteristic dark, wrinkled appearance and premium quality. This prized spice adds deep warmth to curries, marinades, and gourmet dishes worldwide. Beyond its culinary appeal, Ceylon black pepper is highly valued for its powerful antioxidant properties and ability to aid digestion.
